# SEO master checklist: scheduled publishing, owner-gated trust layer, 14 landing pages and 23 queued articles

The change covers every [Code] item and the code half of every [Dono] item in `SEO-CHECKLIST.md`. Posts with a future `published` date are hidden from every surface until their IST day, with hourly ISR. Owner-data features (GA4, Calendly, verification meta, IndexNow, authors, founder, testimonials, logos, proof, pricing, geo, map, lead form) render nothing until they are filled in. It adds 7 service, 3 industry and 4 location pages, a cost calculator, a LIMS URS lead magnet and 23 articles queued Tue/Fri from 2026-10-06 to 2026-12-22, and brings 10 existing posts up to the article rules. The diff runs from `origin/master` (881ada6) through local commit 4fe1cab plus the uncommitted working tree.

Watch for: the "existing articles audit" item is ticked although 20 of 26 pre-existing posts (by the checklist's own note) are still under the 1,500-word rule it lists (confirmed). One queued post goes live with only 2 working in-body links (confirmed). Every sitemap URL now reports a new lastmod each hour (confirmed).

**Verdict**: NEEDS_CHANGES

## High-level view

Scheduling goes through one gate. `todayIST()` and `isPublished()` are evaluated per render, and every consumer (listing, post page, OG image, sitemap, llms.txt, home teaser, related links, landing `related`, in-body `PostLink`) reads the published-only functions. The served normal build bears this out: scheduled slugs and their OG images 404, and they are absent from the sitemap (72 URLs) and llms.txt. A `CONTENT_NOW=2026-12-31` build crawls 95 pages with 0 errors and 0 warnings. No raw `<Link>` points at a scheduled slug. A post goes live on its date only if the host supports ISR, and that is documented for the owner.

The owner-gated layer fails closed. With no data or env set, served HTML has no GA script, no verification meta, no Calendly button, no Review, GeoCoordinates, openingHours or iframe, and `/indexnow.txt` and `/about/founder` both return 404. `trust.ts` and `authors.ts` ship empty, with commented examples only.

On content integrity, no invented data turned up in the new pages or posts. Every price band in the calculator and the new cost, MVP, AI-cost and Next.js-vs-WordPress posts traces to figures already published at the base commit. The salary post reuses the academy hub's bands, labelled indicative with "check current job listings". The PVgenix and LIMS mentions repeat pre-existing `/work` claims. No published date changed. `updated: 2026-10-04` appears only on the 10 posts whose text really changed, and the other 16 got metadata or formatting only. No URL or slug was removed.

The checklist is mostly honest: CWV is left `[ ]` with Lighthouse figures, and owner items are `[~]`. One `[x]` contradicts its own evidence, and two smaller gaps sit in content-link timing and the sitemap.

### Existing-article audit tick vs the 1,500-word rule

The Phase 3 item "Existing articles ko article rules ke hisaab se audit: 1,500–2,500 words, answer pehle, comparison table, FAQ…" is `[x]`. Its own note concedes "20 posts ki sirf article body 1,500 se kam (crawler words 1,581+)". The crawler's Words column counts all of `<main>`, so it overstates the body. Counting `<article>` in the served HTML, which still includes PillarNav and the CTA, gives 1,379 words for `best-programming-language-to-learn-india`, 1,383 for `flutter-developer-hiring-models` and 1,429 for `skills-for-placement-btech-bca-mca` (confirmed). The audit itself happened, but the item names a rule most of these posts don't meet, so `[x]` breaks the "ticks backed by evidence" rule. `[~]` with the existing note is the accurate state.

### Link timing on queued posts

Because `PostLink` hides links to posts that aren't live, a post's live link count on its own publish day depends on which targets are already out. The final crawler evidence was taken at `CONTENT_NOW=2026-12-31`, which hides this. Recomputing per publish date, `gamp-5-category-4-vs-category-5` (2026-10-13) has 4 in-body links: the GAMP 5 guide, `custom-lims-vs-off-the-shelf-lims` (10-23), `what-is-a-validation-package` (11-06) and the CSV service. Only 2 resolve at launch (confirmed). Every other queued post has at least 3 live links on its day. FEAT-006's claim that every post has ≥ 3 live links on its publish day covers #11–#18 only, and this post comes from FEAT-004.

### Sitemap lastmod under ISR

`sitemap.ts` already stamped static and landing pages with `new Date()` at the base commit, which used to mean build time. With the new `revalidate = 3600` the timestamp moves forward every hour for about 45 URLs whose content hasn't changed (confirmed in code). Google tends to ignore lastmod once it proves unreliable, and that would also weaken the accurate `updated ?? published` dates on posts.

### Verification run for this review

The review re-ran these checks: `npm run lint` (0 errors, 2 pre-existing `<img>` warnings in OG files) and `npm run build` (ISR 1h on every listed route, `dynamicParams` on the post and OG routes). It also served `next start -p 3100` and crawled it (72 pages, 0/0/1), checked the empty-state served HTML, and ran a `CONTENT_NOW=2026-12-31` build and crawl (95 pages, 0/0/1). The tree was left on a normal build with the server stopped. The GA4, lead-form and AuthorBox paths were not exercised with real values, as FEAT-007 also states.
